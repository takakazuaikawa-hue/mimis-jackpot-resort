Coins Sound Effects Library by **Little Robot Sound Factory**.

- Author: http://www.littlerobotsoundfactory.com
- Original publication: https://opengameart.org/content/coins-sound-effects-library
- License: **Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)** — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- Download: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Coins%20Sound%20Library.zip

The four `payout-*.wav` files are edited from Coins_Few_21, Coins_Several_15,
Coins_Pouring_00 and Coins_Pouring_05 respectively. Changes: 85 Hz high-pass,
-2.5 dB treble shelf at 3500 Hz, silence trimming where applicable, level
adjustment to a -3 dBFS peak, 1 ms opening / 5 ms ending edge fades and
conversion to 48 kHz / 16-bit PCM. Original pitch and natural settling tails
are retained. No endorsement by the original author is implied.
Retain this attribution with distributed derivatives. Exact source and edited
file hashes, timing and gain are recorded in the source workspace manifest.json.
